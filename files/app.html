04 — App · work in progress

All your data, in one place.

Your watch knows your sleep, another app has your training, a PDF in your email has your blood work. The app pulls it together so you can actually look at it — and so that running a hack from this site means something more than a vague feeling six weeks later.

Get early access See what's built

Honest status

It isn't finished, and there's no download link yet. Connections and the timeline work; experiments and labs are being built now. Early access means a rough build and a direct line to complain about it.

What it does

Four jobs, nothing more.

It's a place to keep and read your own numbers. It won't score you, rank you against strangers, or tell you what's wrong with you.

01 · Connect

Everything in one timeline

Bring in what you already record and stop switching between apps to compare two things.

  • Garmin, Apple Health, Oura, WhoopSleep, heart rate, training load.
  • Manual logFor the things no device measures.
  • CSV importBring years of history with you.
02 · Run

Follow a hack properly

Pick a hack, fix the duration and the one metric you'll watch before you start — the part everybody skips.

  • Baseline firstTwo weeks of normal before you change anything.
  • One metricChosen up front, so you can't move the goalposts.
  • An end dateThe app asks you what happened, either way.
03 · Keep

Blood work that stays readable

Type a panel in once and see it next to the rest of your history instead of hunting through old PDFs.

  • Panels over timeThe same marker, year after year.
  • Your own reference notesWhat your doctor actually said.
  • No interpretationWe show the numbers; the reading is between you and a clinician.

Your data

Three promises we'd rather be held to.

01

Export whenever

Everything you put in comes out as plain files, without asking anyone. Leaving should be easy or the rest doesn't count.

02

Never sold

Health data isn't an advertising asset. It doesn't get sold, shared with data brokers, or used to train anything.

03

No diagnosis

The app shows you your numbers. It won't tell you what they mean about your health, because that's a doctor's job and not a screen's.

Early access is rough on purpose.

Small groups, one at a time, so the feedback is real. Ask in the Discord and you'll be in the next one.
