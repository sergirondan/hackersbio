How we work

Curious, not certain.

Three rules that keep this useful. They also explain what you won't find here.

01

Say how sure we are

Every hack carries its own confidence, in words rather than a made-up score. When the honest answer is "nobody knows yet", that's what it says.

02

One person is a story, not proof

What worked for one body is worth reading and worth trying. It isn't evidence, and we never dress it up as such.

03

Write up the failures too

The hacks that did nothing are the most useful pages on the site, and the rarest anywhere else. They stay published.
