02 — Community

The arguing is the method.

One person trying something is an anecdote. Two hundred people trying it, writing down what happened and picking holes in each other's reasoning is the closest we get to knowing. It lives in two places: a Discord organised by topic, and a WhatsApp group for the day-to-day.

Join the Discord WhatsApp group

Where things happen

  • Discord — one channel per topic, threads that stay searchable, and the monthly test.
  • WhatsApp — a low-volume group for quick questions and "look at this paper" moments.
  • The notes — whatever gets settled ends up rewriting a hack.

This month

Caffeine timing, run together.

Every month the group picks one hack and runs it at the same time, with the same protocol and the same metric. Not a trial — nobody's blinded and everybody knows what they're taking — but a hundred people doing the same thing beats one person guessing.

House rules

Four things we hold to

  • Bring the source"I read somewhere" is fine as a question, not as an answer.
  • Say how sure you areGuessing out loud is welcome. Guessing quietly isn't.
  • No sellingNo affiliate links, no codes, no supplement stacks for sale.
  • Nobody diagnoses anybodySymptoms and medication go to a doctor, not to a chat.

How a note changes

From a message to a rewrite.

01

Someone brings a paper

In #corrections, with a link and a line on what it changes. That's the whole barrier to entry.

02

The channel picks at it

Sample size, funding, whether it actually contradicts the note or just adds nuance. This part takes days, not minutes.

03

The note gets rewritten

Credited by name, with the old version still visible. Ninety-six of them so far.
